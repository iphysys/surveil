What this shows

A scripted, fully synthetic intruder-detection scenario: a subject enters a monitored zone, gets flagged, escalates, then resolves — looping continuously. Nothing here comes from a real camera, radar, or sensor.

How it works

placeholder copy

A small backend server advances through a fixed timeline and exposes the current detection event over HTTP. The frontend polls that feed and drives the scene and HUD directly from it — colors, pulse rate, and ring timing all come from the same synthetic event, never an independent animation clock. All data is synthetic.
